Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Skills ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and disclosed by the Skills application.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

  • Usage Data: We collect anonymous usage data (e.g., screens visited, features used) via PostHog to improve the app.
  • Local Data: Your habits, entries, notes, streaks, and Focus session history are stored locally on your device with SwiftData, in an app group shared with the Skills widget. There is no account and no cloud sync: iCloud sync is disabled.
  • Apple Health: Health data is read only, one metric at a time, and only after you allow it. Skills never writes to Health, and no Health value and no habit content is ever sent to analytics.
  • Purchase Information: If you make an in-app purchase, Apple and RevenueCat securely process the transaction. We do not have access to your payment details.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the application.
  • To analyze usage trends and track crash reports so we can fix bugs.
  • To manage your subscription status for Premium access.

3. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

The app uses the following third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • PostHog (Analytics) — opt-in, explicit events only, with no session replay and no autocapture.
  • RevenueCat (Purchase management), together with Apple's App Store.

Skills has no backend of its own. Your habit data never leaves your device.

4. DATA RETENTION & DELETION

Because your habit data and history are stored locally, you can export it as CSV, delete a habit in the app, or remove everything at once by uninstalling the app.

5. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. CONTACT US

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: jiyuusoftware@gmail.com
